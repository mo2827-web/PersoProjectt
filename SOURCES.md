# Score sources and limits

## What the Quality Signals Score means

This is an evidence-completeness score, not a laboratory quality grade or lifespan prediction. It rewards only four facts that are visible on the checked product page. The result screen shows which of these points were earned for that specific page:

| Visible fact | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Material composition is disclosed | 30 |
| Care instructions are disclosed | 20 |
| Construction detail is disclosed | 25 |
| Origin, traceability, or other transparency evidence is disclosed | 25 |

The app does not award or remove points simply because a product includes a natural or synthetic fiber. Fiber content alone cannot establish garment quality, fabric weight, knit density, finishing, pilling resistance, or actual lifespan. Missing facts stay visible under **Unknown factors** and can create a **Transparency Concern**.

## Fabric-property context

The first section of a result gives general reference context for disclosed wool and cotton. It is not a score input and does not make a quality claim about that garment. Blends, yarn type, fabric construction, finishing, and garment design can substantially change real-world performance.

## References

- [FTC: Apparel and labeling](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/topics/tools-consumers/apparel-labeling) — explains that fiber content, country of origin, and marketer identity are key apparel-label disclosures.
- [FTC: Care Labeling Rule](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/care-labeling-textile-wearing-apparel-certain-piece-goods-text) — explains required care information and the reasonable basis required for care instructions.
- [Woolmark: What is wool fibre?](https://www.woolmark.com/fibre/what-is-the-wool-fibre/) — provides fiber-specific context for wool properties. It is not used to rate every wool garment as better than another material.
- [CottonWorks: Fibers booklet](https://cottonworks.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Fibers_Booklet_edited-1.pdf) — discusses cotton’s moisture absorption, strength, and limits such as lower wrinkle recovery; this general context does not rate a specific garment.

## Limits

The score uses visible page information only. It does not test the garment, verify brand claims, assess labor or environmental practices, or promise durability.
