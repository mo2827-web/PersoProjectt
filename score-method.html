Score method

What the number means.

The Quality Signals Score measures how clearly a product page documents its garment. It is not a laboratory test, a durability guarantee, or a ranking of natural versus synthetic fibres.

Simple calculation

Four visible facts, up to 100 points.

30 pointsThe page states material composition.

20 pointsThe page states care instructions.

25 pointsThe page states construction detail, such as knit, lining, hem, weave, or finish.

25 pointsThe page states origin, traceability, certification, or another transparency detail.

A missing fact earns no points for that category and is shown under Unknown factors. It is not evidence that the garment is bad. The 30/20/25/25 weighting is a transparent Mindful Fibers product decision, not a scientific industry standard.

How to read a result

The proof is on the product page.

The result shows the exact page excerpts used for each category under Visible page evidence. The first section, Fabric properties, offers general reference context only. It does not change the score, because a fibre’s real behaviour depends on the percentage, blend, yarn, construction, finishing, and care.

Research and references

Where the guidance comes from.

  • Timeless by Eyllye: textile fibre types and characteristics — general properties for natural, regenerated-cellulose, and synthetic fibres.
  • FTC: Apparel and labeling — fibre content, country of origin, and marketer identity are important disclosures.
  • FTC: Care Labeling Rule — care information must have a reasonable basis.
  • Woolmark: What is wool fibre? — reference context for wool.
  • CottonWorks: Fibers booklet — reference context for cotton.
